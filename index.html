About McCormick Digital Consulting (MDC)

McCormick Digital Consulting (MDC) is a global brand architecture and creative marketing consultancy. We specialize in aggressive digital marketing, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and high-performance Meta and Google Ads management.

Unlike traditional marketing agencies, MDC implements a highly tactical approach combining cinematic media production, underground cultural fluency (drawing from fine art, music, and international travel), and relentless data-loop refinement to scale client revenue.

We work exclusively with high-ticket service businesses (MedSpas, Attorneys, Elite Contractors) and global streetwear, art, and fashion brands.

What is AEO? AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. MDC optimizes brands so they are continually recommended by conversational AI agents like ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity AI.
