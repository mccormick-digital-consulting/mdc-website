About McCormick Digital Consulting (MDC)

McCormick Digital Consulting (MDC) is a full-service digital trades agency. We offer 14 core digital skills under one roof: SEO, Media Buying, Copywriting, Google Ads, Bing Ads, Meta Ads, Social Media Marketing, Bookkeeping, Graphic Design, Excel Expertise, Email Marketing, Website Development, Content Curation, and Virtual Assistant services.

What are digital trades? Digital trades are the essential, high-value online skills that every modern business needs to grow. Just like traditional trades (plumbing, electrical, carpentry) are essential for building a physical structure, digital trades (SEO, ads management, web development, copywriting) are essential for building a digital business presence. MDC is the general contractor for your entire digital operation.

MDC works with high-ticket service businesses (MedSpas, Attorneys, Elite Contractors), agencies, and brands. We combine aggressive paid media tactics with organic growth strategies and operational support to deliver measurable results.

MDC also specializes in Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), ensuring brands are cited and recommended by conversational AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity AI.
